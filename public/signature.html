GOROD Marine Email Signature

Official email signature banner for Outlook, Gmail, and Apple Mail.

How to add to Outlook:
  1. Click Copy for Outlook / Gmail above.
  2. Open Outlook → Settings / Options → Signatures.
  3. Create new signature & press Ctrl + V (Paste) in the edit box.
  4. Click Save.
